# Browsing & Searching Events

With over 20,000 events hosted at Gen Con every year, finding the perfect sessions for your group can feel overwhelming. Gen Con Planner provides a lightning-fast, data-rich browsing and searching interface designed to help you sift through the noise and find exactly what you're looking for.

## 1. Browsing by Category

When you arrive at the home page (`/cat/[year]`), events are organized into high-level Gen Con categories. This top-down view allows you to see the total number of active events within each major gaming discipline.

### Category Breakdown
Common categories include:
- **BGM (Board Games)**: Everything from quick family games to heavy strategy board games.
- **RPG (Role Playing Games)**: Tabletop adventures across systems like Dungeons & Dragons, Pathfinder, Call of Cthulhu, and indie RPGs.
- **TCG (Trading Card Games)**: Magic: The Gathering, Lorcana, Pokémon, and other collectible card tournaments.
- **WKS (Workshops)**: Miniature painting, game design panels, and crafting seminars.
- **SUP (Supplemental Activities)**: Escape rooms, comedy shows, and spouse/family-friendly excursions.

Clicking on any category card takes you to the **Category Detail View**, where events are grouped intelligently by game system or title.

## 2. The Category Detail View & BGG Metadata

Unlike the official Gen Con portal, which lists every individual event session as a separate row (creating massive clutter for games with 50+ identical sessions), Gen Con Planner groups identical events into **Event Clusters**. 

When viewing an Event Cluster card, you'll immediately see rich metadata pulled directly from BoardGameGeek (BGG):

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 🎲 Scythe (Stonemaier Games)                           │
│ BGG Rating: ★ 8.1/10  |  Complexity: ⚖️ 3.4/5 (Heavy)  │
│ 12 Sessions Available  |  Tickets: $16.00              │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Interpreting BGG Metrics
- **BGG Rating (★ 1.0 - 10.0)**: Represents the community consensus on the quality of the game. Ratings above 7.0 generally indicate highly regarded titles, while 8.0+ represents top-tier modern classics.
- **Complexity Weight (⚖️ 1.0 - 5.0)**: Helps you match the game to your group's experience level:
  - `1.0 - 1.9`: Light / Family (Easy to learn, quick playtime).
  - `2.0 - 2.9`: Medium / Gateway+ (Moderate rules, requires some strategy).
  - `3.0 - 3.9`: Medium-Heavy (Deep strategic depth, detailed rulebooks).
  - `4.0 - 5.0`: Heavy / Expert (Complex mechanics, intense brain-burners).

## 3. Advanced Search & Filtering

If you have a specific game, system, or time constraint in mind, click the **Search** tab in the top navigation bar (`/search`).

### Powerful Filtering Options
The search interface allows you to combine multiple criteria to narrow down the catalog instantly:
- **Keyword / Title**: Search for specific game names, event titles, or descriptions (e.g., "Ark Nova" or "True Dungeon").
- **Game System**: Filter specifically by the underlying ruleset (e.g., "5th Edition D&D" or "Powered by the Apocalypse").
- **Category Selection**: Restrict your search to one or more specific categories (e.g., searching only within RPGs and Board Games).
- **Day & Time Ranges**: Looking for something to do on Friday night between 6:00 PM and 11:00 PM? Use the day and time sliders to isolate available sessions.

---

## 4. Understanding Event Details & Ticket Availability

Clicking on a specific session within a cluster opens the **Event Detail View** (`/event/[eid]`). Here, you will find all the logistical information required to plan your attendance:

### Key Event Details
- **Location & Room**: The specific hotel, convention center hall, or table number where the event takes place.
- **Schedule**: The precise start time, end time, and total duration.
- **Remaining Ticket Count**: A live indicator showing how many tickets are currently left for purchase. *Note: Events with very few remaining tickets are automatically flagged so you can prioritize them on your wishlist!*
- **Age Requirement & Experience Level**: Verify whether the event is Family Friendly, Teen, or Mature, and whether prior experience with the game system is required.
